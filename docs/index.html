How to play

A room, a letter, a race.

01

Open a room

Sign in with Google or an email, create a room and send your friends its short code. They join with it from the app or the browser.

02

Race the letter

Press start and a letter is drawn for everyone at once. Fill in a person, an animal and a thing that start with it, then hit stop.

03

Score it together

Each answer is worth 0, 5 or 10, as you agree around the table. Totals update live for every player, round after round.
